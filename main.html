Superintelligence — Nick Bostrom

                        

                            Essential reading on AI alignment and existential
                            risk. Rigorous analysis of what happens when
                            machines surpass human intelligence.
                        

                        

                            Philosophy essayUK
                        

                        

                            

                                I used to treat “progress” as an automatic good;
                                this made me pace my optimism. Now when a system
                                promises scale, I ask three calm questions: who
                                stays in control if it works, how failure
                                spreads when it doesn’t, and what off‑switch
                                actually exists. It’s a book I read in quiet
                                evenings — two pages can seed a week of thinking
                                — and it left me more reflective than alarmed.
                                I’m kinder to uncertainty now: it’s fine not to
                                know, as long as I can name the bet I’m making.
